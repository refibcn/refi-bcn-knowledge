summary: 'CGT Catalunya illustrates a federated, self-managed union governance model: a Permanent Secretariat coordinates but does not command; real authority sits in Local and Sectoral Federations bound by Congress, Plenary, and Union Conference agreements. The model pairs direct-democracy decision-making with a broad action mandate that extends beyond workplace bargaining into ecological/ecosocial transition, feminist and LGBTIQ+ organizing, legal defense, and occupational health — i.e. labor organizing treated as one strand of a wider social-movement federation rather than a narrow bargaining unit.'
