summary: 'A model for municipal governance that reorients a city''s economy around planetary and human well-being rather than continued economic growth, pioneered as a formal, resourced local-government practice by Girona (Catalonia) from early 2024 (see the "Girona Postcreixentista" resource). Structurally it combines: a critical, cross-department review of urban policy through a growth-dependency lens; relocalized production using biomaterials; distributed wealth via wealth taxes coordinated across municipalities; resource-flow awareness linking the city to its hinterland and bioregion; work/profit-sharing (more jobs, fewer hours); "collective luxury" valued over individual accumulation; and ecological-economics education grounded in planetary limits. It is presented as a bridge between academic degrowth theory and practical municipal implementation, and as a replicable template for other Catalan municipalities.'
