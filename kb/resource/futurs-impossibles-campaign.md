Decentralised Catalan campaign begun September 2022, impulsed by Xarxa d'Economia Solidària (XES), Coòpolis and Observatori del Deute en la Globalització, joined by 50+ collectives across the ESS (social & solidarity economy) ecosystem, climate-justice, feminist and migrant-justice movements. Ran five open plenaries plus working groups (communication, content, logistics, action & alliances) that built up to the "Fòrum per la Transició Ecosocial" (Barcelona, 24–25 Feb 2023), the campaign's flagship convening event.
