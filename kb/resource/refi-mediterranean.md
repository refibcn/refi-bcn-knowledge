A "constellation" (non-hierarchical) network of bioregional regenerative- finance nodes across the Mediterranean basin, which ReFi Barcelona describes itself as having helped catalyze. Per this article, active nodes (as of the article's vintage) were: ReFi Barcelona (Catalonia — cooperative governance, technical infra), ReFi Sicilia (Sicily — rural regeneration, agroforestry), ReFi Lisboa (Portugal — marine regeneration, blue economy), ReFi PACA (Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur — Alpine-Mediterranean transition, water systems), and Earthist (Türkiye — earthquake regeneration, community resilience). Collaboration mechanisms cited: knowledge commoning (shared practices database, learning exchanges), resource pooling (quadratic funding, investment circles, joint grants), market network development (certification, exchange platforms, complementary currencies), and governance collaboration (a "Mediterranean ReFi Council", shared policy framework).
