summary: '"Meaningful Occupation" is Chapter#2''s core organizing concept: occupation (paid or unpaid activity, most often framed through entrepreneurship) that carries personal significance and forms part of an individual''s personal and social identity. Chapter#2 operationalizes it via a "Triangle Model" sitting at the intersection of three elements — Meaningful Occupation, Lean Methodologies (rapid prototyping, customer feedback, iterative improvement), and Social Economy (an economic model prioritizing social and environmental benefit alongside financial sustainability). The framework is applied mainly to migrant/refugee labour and social inclusion programs (entrepreneurship accelerators, hackathons, consultancy) in Catalonia.'
audience: ReFi BCN researchers/practitioners mapping bioregional social-economy and labour-inclusion frameworks in Catalunya; useful as a comparison point against ReFi/regen framings of "livelihood" or "right relationship to work."
