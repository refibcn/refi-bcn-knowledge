Worker cooperative (Apunta Teatre SCCL, constituted 2003 as a theatre company) that in 2018 took over management of the pre-existing Versus Teatre venue (open since 1995, carrer de Castillejos 179, 08013 Barcelona) and refounded it as Sala Versus Glòries — 11 worker-members, 8 collaborating members. Contact: info@salaversusglories.cat, 932 323 184. Produces its own shows, programmes other companies, backs local playwrights and non-commercial theatre, favours socially critical work (Franco-era repression, patriarchal oppression, popular memory, business corruption, urban loneliness). Inter-cooperates with the Orquestra Simfònica del Vallès (part of the Clade cooperative group) and Escola de Música del Palau. Link/contact info unverified in this pass — see coverage signal.
