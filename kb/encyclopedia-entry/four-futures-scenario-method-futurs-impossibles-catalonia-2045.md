The four scenarios named in source: (1) authoritarian protection of the privileged, (2) corporate elite domination with deep inequalities, (3) green-sensitive welfare state, (4) sovereign, democratic, just and ecological territories (the desired one). Method also produced an "ESS Transition Guide" for organizations in the social economy. Originated in connection with the 2023 Forum for Ecosocial Transition per the source page; being shared at European level per "Recent Developments".
