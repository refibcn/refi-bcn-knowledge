World's-first "post-growth municipality" initiative, launched via a formal memorandum of understanding signed early 2024 by Girona City Council with Research & Degrowth International Association, Dark Matter Labs, and LEQUIA (University of Girona) as partners (each has its own entity page elsewhere in this corpus — not duplicated here). Multi-phase methodology: (1) critical review of municipal policy across departments from a growth-dependency angle, (2) outline proposals across sectors/districts, (3) collaborative refinement with citizens/businesses/public sector (2024-2027 per source), (4) a just eco-social transition plan for Girona. Vision includes relocalized production, distributed wealth via inter-municipal wealth taxes, resource-flow awareness linking the city to its hinterland and Catalonia, shared work/profit, and ecological-economics education. Received media coverage in NacióDigital, Climática, Ara, The Atlantic, and El Crític (citations only, not modeled as resources here). Second project URL: https://darkmatterlabs.org/feed/fostering-a-post-growth-municipality-in-girona.
