summary: Dark Matter Labs' umbrella framing for economic systems organized around the flourishing of all life rather than extraction and accumulation. Rests on three worldview shifts — (1) rooted in the full web of life (from violence/scarcity/separation to thriving planetary community), (2) grounded in a non-bounded understanding of value (from extractive profit-driven goals to entangled, intergenerational value systems), (3) enabled by technological ecosystems of care (from utilitarian "othering" of technology to animistic interfaces of wisdom and care) — and pursued through concrete "Economic Options" the source describes as "bold directional aspirations" (e.g. city-scale tree canopies, community endowments, resilient bioregional food systems, self-owning houses).
audience: Ecosystem mappers, funders, and org-design collaborators evaluating post-growth / regenerative-economy frameworks that ReFi BCN's bioregional finance work sits adjacent to.
known_tensions:
  - Explicitly aspirational/directional rather than a tested operating model — the source itself labels the "Economic Options" as "bold directional aspirations," not proven interventions.
  - Depends on redefining value and ownership at a civilizational scale, which is hard to reconcile with the legal/financial infrastructure ReFi BCN and its partners actually operate within today.
