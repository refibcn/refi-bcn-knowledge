summary: An organizational pattern, exemplified in the source by Ecohub, in which multiple independent cooperative entities (in Ecohub's case nine, comprising 100+ professionals) federate under one shared mission and brand ("pol cooperatiu") to offer combined services — accompaniment, facilitation, knowledge transfer, resource provision — to citizens, organizations, businesses, and public administrations pursuing a strategic theme (here, "just and democratic ecological transitions"). The model bundles multidisciplinary, multi-entity capacity under one front door rather than each entity contracting separately, and typically pairs with an explicit theoretical framework (Ecohub uses Doughnut Economics) to give the bundle a shared analytic lens.
