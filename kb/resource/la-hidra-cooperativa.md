Described in the source only via the "Comunalitats Urbanes" connections row: a research cooperative focused on commons and public-commons partnerships, coordinating the central "Oficina de Suport" (support office) for the Generalitat's Comunalitats Urbanes program (training, methodology, evaluation for ~22 local community consortiums). Source states its members "have roots in the Fundación de los Comunes" (spelled Fundació dels Comuns in Catalan), likened in-text to a "Commons Agency."
