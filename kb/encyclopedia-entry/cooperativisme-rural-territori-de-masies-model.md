summary: 'Rural cooperativism as practiced by Territori de Masies, sccl: an economic and social model based on mutual aid, democratic participation, and sustainable local development, organized as a non-profit mixed cooperative (consumption, services, and agricultural) that pools neighbors, associations, and local businesses of a defined rural territory (here, baix Solsonès) to jointly produce, market, and advocate for a place-based way of life. The page frames this against the concepts "Pagès/Pagesa" (traditional Catalan rural farmer/agricultural worker) and "Territori de Masies" (territory of farmhouses — traditional Catalan rural architecture and settlement pattern), treating the cooperative structure as the institutional vehicle for keeping a farmhouse-based rural landscape economically viable.'
audience: ReFi BCN researchers mapping bioregional-finance / cooperative-model precedents in Catalunya; anyone scoping how a rural-heritage cooperative bundles production, tourism, energy, and cultural-preservation activities under one legal-cooperative umbrella.
known_tensions:
  - Cultural preservation vs. tourism monetization — the same cooperative runs a "Responsible Tourism Decalogue" and paid "Territory Routes" alongside a stated mission to protect (not commodify) traditional rural life; the source does not reconcile this tension.
  - Small-cooperative sustainability vs. institutional dependency — the model leans on multiple public-sector supporters (Generalitat, SEPE, Diputació de Lleida) rather than being fully self-sustaining, which cuts against the "mutual aid / self-determination" framing.
