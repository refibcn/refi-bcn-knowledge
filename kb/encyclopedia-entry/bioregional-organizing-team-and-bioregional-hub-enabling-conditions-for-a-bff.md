summary: 'The BioFi framework''s precondition for a Bioregional Financing Facility (BFF): a Bioregional Organizing Team is the base layer of trusting relationships, coherence, and coordination capacity among community leaders taking shared responsibility for stewarding organizing, weaving, and activation in a bioregion — it may be an existing respected community org or a fresh decentralized network of individuals/small groups; what matters is relationship quality, not form. Creating an explicit Bioregional Regeneration Strategy (assessing present-day health, articulating a shared vision, aligning on values/key initiatives) is recommended as the exercise that both builds this trust/coherence and tests readiness for bigger steps. Once that foundation exists, a Bioregional Hub can be stood up to strengthen the collaboration network: stewarding co-creation/implementation of the Regeneration Strategy, ongoing systems mapping/research, capacity building, incubating regenerative business cases, transparent progress tracking, and interfacing with global networks of bioregional hubs/learning centers. Organizing Team, Hub, and BFF are framed as the "three synergistic legs of a stool" that supports bioregional regeneration.'
audience: Bioregional organizers assessing readiness to launch coordination infrastructure before attempting a financing facility.
