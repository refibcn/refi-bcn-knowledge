summary: 'A named urban-planning model — developed by FEUT / Salvador Rueda — that groups several city blocks together, restricting through-traffic to the perimeter and freeing the interior as pedestrian-oriented public space. In the source''s framing it bundles four components: mobility & public-space reclamation, ecosystemic (nature-based) urban design, decentralization of social services into distributed community amenities, and integrated transport planning. Piloted in Barcelona and since adapted internationally (cited flagship deployments: Granollers re-naturalization, Málaga maritime-front transformation, Bogotá superblocks plan).'
