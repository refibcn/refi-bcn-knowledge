Cooperative of live-event technicians (Larry's SCCL, carrer de la Noguera Pallaresa 10, baixos, 08014 Barcelona; 4 worker-members), operating since 2005, sharing knowledge, clients and equipment; contact: larrys@larrys.cat, 608 965 230. Specialises in production, technical direction, machinery, sound and lighting for events across Catalunya and the rest of Spain; shares a warehouse in Sant Quirze del Vallès with another sound company and a cultural-events producer for shared equipment use, and hosts a small rehearsal theatre adjacent to the warehouse for touring bands/companies. Has an agreement with the Centre de Recursos per Associacions Juvenils (CRAJ) offering affordable rental to member entities.
