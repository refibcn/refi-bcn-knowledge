Territori de Masies, sccl (Societat Cooperativa Catalana Limitada) — rural non-profit mixed cooperative (consumption, services, agricultural) formed by neighbors, associations, and businesses of the baix Solsonès region (comarca Solsonès, província Lleida): Llanera, La Molsosa, Pinós, Riner, Llobera, Pinell de Solsonès, Clariana de Cardener, plus an extended area in Castelltallat (Bages). Mission: promote, advocate for, and protect the ways of life, landscapes, and rural life of the villages — "per viure a pagès n'hem de poder viure!" (to live as rural people, we must be able to make a living). Flagship products: ecological flour, territory croquettes, Christmas lots, territory pizza. Major initiatives beyond the flagship products: "Coop de Territori" (multi-cooperative collaboration, see separate resource), "Bassadòria" environmental-education project, "Coop de Sol" collective solar-panel purchase for energy sovereignty, "Amb ulls de Dones" (women's-perspective project), a responsible-tourism decalogue, and a bi-annual magazine (18 issues). Headquarters: Santuari del Miracle, 25287 El Miracle, Riner (Solsonès); open Mon–Fri 9AM–2PM. Institutional supporters named on the page: Generalitat de Catalunya, Dep. d'Empresa i Treball, SEPE, Turisme Solsonès, Consell Comarcal Solsonès, Diputació de Lleida.
