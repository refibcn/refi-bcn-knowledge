summary: 'ReFi BCN''s staged process for turning member/community ideas into implemented initiatives. Five categories of eligible initiatives are named (knowledge-commons, community-building, technical, economic, governance-innovation), each running through five stages — Ideation, Proposal, Review, Implementation, Evaluation — with a stated purpose, process, requirements, and who-can-participate rule per stage. Approval authority is tiered by budget/duration: micro (under €500, under 3 months) decided by the relevant working group; small (€500-2,000, 3-6 months) reviewed by the working group and approved by the Coordination Circle; medium (€2,000-5,000, 6-12 months) reviewed by the Coordination Circle and approved by the General Assembly; large (over €5,000, over 12 months) requiring a comprehensive package, a review period of at least one month, and General Assembly approval with 75% consensus. Initiatives are expected to align with five named strategic priorities and to feed a "knowledge commons" documentation/learning loop, with "failure reports" explicitly named as a valued output.'
audience: ReFi BCN members and community participants wanting to propose or join an initiative; working-group and Coordination Circle members applying the approval thresholds.
