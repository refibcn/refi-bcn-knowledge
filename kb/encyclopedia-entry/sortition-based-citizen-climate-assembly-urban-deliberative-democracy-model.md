summary: 'A replicable municipal model for climate policymaking: a demographically representative body of residents, selected by stratified lottery (sortition) rather than election, deliberates over a fixed cycle and produces vote-ready policy proposals for the city government. As distilled from Barcelona''s Assemblea Ciutadana pel Clima (2022-2023), the model''s reusable components are: (1) robust random selection for demographic legitimacy, (2) a layered evidence cycle (expert talks + lived-experience testimony) that levels the knowledge field before deliberation, (3) small working groups nested in a handful of thematic axes producing structured proposal sheets (problem / beneficiaries / actions), and (4) a built-in follow-up architecture (a standing oversight commission plus open data) guarding against participatory "hit-and-run" — i.e. proposals that get adopted with fanfare and then quietly dropped.'
