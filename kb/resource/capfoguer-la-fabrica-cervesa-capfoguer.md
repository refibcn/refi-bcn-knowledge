Craft-beer and restaurant worker cooperative (La Fàbrica Cervesa Capfoguer SCCL, avinguda de Santa Coloma 104, 08922 Santa Coloma de Gramenet; 9 worker-members), founded 2008 at the Ateneu Popular Júlia Romera, now sharing space with Ca La Sisqueta. Contact: info@capfoguer.com, 935 662 566. Brews and serves four beer varieties on-site, supplies beer for cultural/music events across Catalunya (e.g. the Literal book fair), and runs a horizontally-organised restaurant emphasising seasonal, local cooking. Practices intercooperation, decent work, externality control, training and rootedness in the territory. One of ten cooperatives in the Pol Cooperatiu del Besòs, where it drives "La Popular del Besòs" — a cooperative, organic, locally-rooted beer.
