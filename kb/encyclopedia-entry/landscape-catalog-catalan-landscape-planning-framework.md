summary: 'A methodology, developed and maintained by the Observatori del Paisatge de Catalunya, for regional landscape planning: systematic per-territory landscape inventories ("Landscape Catalogs") that classify distinct "Landscape Units," set participatory "Landscape Quality Objectives" for protection/management/improvement, and feed "Landscape Directives" (legal planning instruments) plus local charters. Combines ecological, cultural, and perceptual criteria and is meant to be operationalized through municipal/regional policy, not just documented.'
