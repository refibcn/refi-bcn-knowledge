Catalan music worker cooperative (PPF! SCCL, avinguda dels Dolors 17, 08243 Manresa; 3 worker-members) born in Barcelona in 1997 and later consolidated in Manresa. Contact: xavi@ppf.cat, 938 787 488. Runs a record label with 100+ releases and an artist management/booking office (Obrint Pas, La Raíz, ZOO, Feliu Ventura, KOP, Pirat's Sound Sistema, Auxili, among others), and handles artistic direction/production of the Circuit Estepa Mediterrània (the official OFF section of the Fira Mediterrània de Manresa). Drove the construction of Manresa's Sala Stroika (open since 2011), which the cooperative co-runs; coordinates the Catalunya Central node of the Xarxa de Cases de la Música de Catalunya (alongside nodes in Mataró, Girona-Salt, l'Hospitalet de Llobregat and Terrassa).
