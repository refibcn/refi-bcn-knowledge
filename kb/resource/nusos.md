Nusos Activitats Científiques i Culturals SCCL (constituted 2013), Barcelona. Worker cooperative facilitating public access to scientific and cultural knowledge, centered on scientific and environmental education, with an explicit "democratització del coneixement" (democratization of knowledge) practice — including training its own worker-members so they can contribute to the cooperative's and the wider collectivity's development. Runs workshops, guided visits, exhibitions, talks, and documentation work for cultural/research institutions, conservation entities, and schools. Member of Xarxa d'Economia Solidària (XES) and Federació de Cooperatives de Treball de Catalunya (FCTC), sòcia of Coop57, and part of Grup Cooperatiu ECOS (~20 intercooperating cooperatives spanning property administration, law, food, data analysis, architecture, insurance, communication, journalism, and technology).
