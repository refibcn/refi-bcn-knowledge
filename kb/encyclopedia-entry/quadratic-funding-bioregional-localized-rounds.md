page_type: concept
summary: Quadratic Funding (QF) is a resource-distribution mechanism where a matching pool amplifies many small individual donations more than one large donation to the same project, so citizen participation (breadth of support) — not just size of any single gift — drives how much matching money a project receives. As applied by ReFi-aligned "localized rounds" (place-based QF programs run by bioregional or city-scale communities rather than a single global platform), it is framed as extending cooperative "one member, one vote" democratic principles into funding allocation, and as a way to blend local capital (cooperative banks, foundations, crowdfunding) with international matching capital (e.g. Celo, Gitcoin) to multiply the total pool available to regenerative projects in a given territory.

known_tensions:
  - This entry's source document (a 2025 ReFi Barcelona funding proposal) presents QF's benefits (transparency, democratic decision-making, multiplier effect) without discussing known QF failure modes such as Sybil/collusion attacks on matching pools or the administrative overhead of running a round — the doc is pitch material, not a neutral comparison.
  - The doc conflates two different scales of QF track record — ReFi Barcelona's own participation in the *global* Regen Coordination program (as a grantee/participant) vs. the *localized* rounds it cites as precedent (ReFi Mediterranean, Zazelenimo, Regen Rio de Janeiro, Ma Earth, Regen Coordination Global) — see companion claim-evidence object for the specific figures and their uncertainty.
