summary: 'ReFi BCN''s proposed hybrid organizational model: a legally constituted multi-stakeholder cooperative that "wraps" DAO mechanisms. The cooperative provides legal personhood, contracting capacity, compliance, and local operations; DAO mechanisms (smart contracts, token-based systems) provide transparent governance and global network participation. Framed as solving complementary weaknesses: cooperatives'' limited geographic scale and capital constraints, and DAOs'' legal uncertainty and difficulty with physical/local operations. Token-based systems are described as complementing, not replacing, cooperative membership.'
