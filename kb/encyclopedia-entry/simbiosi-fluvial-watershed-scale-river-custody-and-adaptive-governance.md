Illustrates a repeatable pattern for watershed-scale coordination: place-based ecological scoping at the basin level, commons-based custody arrangements, participatory monitoring, and reframing the human-river relationship ("feeling, observing, and being river") rather than purely extractive or regulatory framing.'
