Non-profit cooperative (Olot, Girona; Garrotxa comarca) that advises municipalities and facilitates participatory evaluations, strategic planning, and formación/debate spaces for community resilience and regenerative design. Publishes a resource section of research documents, analytical methods, and training tools. Operates or co-runs several named sibling bioregional projects (Miceli Social, Núria Social, Simbiosi Fluvial, Fem Garrotxa, Balkar Earth) and partners internationally with Gaia Education ("Bioregioning in Practice" — https://www.gaiaeducation.org/bioregioning-in-practice) and Regenesis Institute ("The Regenerative Practitioner" — https://www.regenerat.es/trp-landing-page-catalonia/) — those sibling projects/programs are not re-noded on this page (see signal 03). Framed by the source as demonstrating BioFi principles (multi-capital, place-based bioregional governance) and aligned with Catalan initiatives Arran de Terra, Eixarcolant, and the broader Bioregionalisme a Catalunya movement.
