Music bar / concert-venue worker cooperative (Koitton Club SCCL, carrer de Rossend Arús 9, 08014 Barcelona; 3 worker-members, 25 collaborating members), operating since 2012 in the Sants neighbourhood. Contact: koittonclub@gmail.com (no phone given). Runs roughly 20 activities a month; co-produces neighbourhood cultural-dynamisation projects (La Fresca, VermutsCoop) and, since 2014, hosts/drives the Comissió de Festes del carrer Rossend Arús, reviving street festivities discontinued since the Civil War. Chairs the Impuls Cooperatiu de Sants, a network of about twenty local community-economy and worker/consumer/housing cooperative projects promoting local cooperative ecosystems.
