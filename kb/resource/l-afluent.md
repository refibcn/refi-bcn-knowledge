Cultural-services worker cooperative (L'Afluent SCCL, Fàbrica de Creació Fabra i Coats, carrer de Sant Adrià 20, 08030 Barcelona; 3 worker-members), created 1 January 2017. Contact: begood@lafluent.com (no phone given). Founding members spent fifteen years running Gràcia's historic Heliogàbal venue before founding the cooperative; currently manage the BeGood concert hall. Part of the cooperative-culture ecosystem forming around the Fàbrica de Creació Fabra i Coats in Sant Andreu; participates in the BAM – Cultura Viva project (design, production, curation) alongside Ateneu l'Harmonia, The Good Good, Quesoni, Koitton Club, Coòpolis BCN, Indigestió, Makea tu vida, Straddle3 and the Xarxa d'Economia Solidària.
