Described in-source as "Spain's first multi-story housing co-op using the grant-of-use model," in Barcelona's Sants neighborhood. Started by future residents in 2012, completed 2018; 28 apartments (home to ~60 people) on city-provided land under a 75-year lease, with ecological design; co-financed by members, ethical banks, and support from Barcelona City and the Catalan Government.
