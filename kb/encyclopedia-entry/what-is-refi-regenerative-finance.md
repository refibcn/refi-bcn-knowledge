summary: Regenerative Finance (ReFi) is a paradigm shift that aims to harmonize financial activity with the well-being of the Earth and communities, rather than extracting profit at the expense of social equity and ecological sustainability. It frames money and financial mechanisms as tools that can support planetary regeneration and holistic community wealth. In practice ReFi spans a broad spectrum of initiatives — from plastic removal and community economies to ocean metagenomics and forest biodiversity — often using Web3/blockchain coordination to connect people and manage resources.
