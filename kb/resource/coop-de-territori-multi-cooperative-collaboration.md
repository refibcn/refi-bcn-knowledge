"Coop de Territori" is a named collaboration project uniting four cooperatives/enterprises committed to territory and agriculture: Territori de Masies Coop, Biolord Coop, L'Arada, and Riuverd (a social-insertion enterprise). No independent URL is given in the source — mentioned only as a sub-initiative on Territori de Masies' own page, so link_status is unresolved rather than active/broken. Treated as its own resource (rather than folded into the Territori de Masies notes) because it is a distinct multi-org collaboration, not a Territori de Masies-only product.
