Catalan territorial cooperative ("Som llavor") recovering wild species and traditional plant varieties via research, education, seed distribution, and cultural activities. HQ Carrer Major 47, 08719 Jorba (Anoia, Barcelona province). Ontology-v1 frontmatter: status active, focus_areas [seeds, biodiversity, agriculture, traditional-knowledge, food-systems], relationship_to_refi_bcn: peer, last_verified 2026-05-06. Organized into territorial nodes (Camp de Tarragona, Urgell, Anoia, others) run by dues-paying members ("socis i sòcies"). Runs recurring events (Gastronomic Journey of Forgotten Plants, Territori Silvestre festival), publishes guides/magazine, sells seeds/tools/preserves, and reports impact metrics (members, informants interviewed, species recovered, people reached). high_risk: cooperative's core method includes "Informant Interviews" — recording traditional/elder ecological knowledge from local communities (TEK-adjacent) — hence the accompanying public-use-boundary.
