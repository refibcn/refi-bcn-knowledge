title: Urban Tsunami (tsunami urbanístic) — Costa Brava territorial-defense framing
type: encyclopedia-entry
page_type: concept
summary: '"Urban tsunami" is the framing term SOS Costa Brava uses for a wave of rapid, large-scale urban and infrastructure development (mass housing, highways, tourism and commercial infrastructure) that overwhelms a coastline''s natural and cultural landscape faster than legal or planning safeguards can respond. The federation anchors its flagship "Construir és Destruir" (Building is Destroying) campaign in the term, arguing that the volume of currently threatened and planned development (per the source page: 30,000+ additional homes threatened, 40,000+ new homes planned, 100+ new urbanizations proposed) would, if built out, permanently erase the Costa Brava''s Mediterranean ecological and cultural identity.'
audience: ReFi BCN practitioners mapping Catalan territorial-defense / anti- speculation advocacy framings against bioregional-finance and BioFi design principles (place-based governance, commons protection, regeneration over extraction).
known_tensions:
  - The term is the federation's own advocacy framing, not a technical planning-law category — no independent threshold or definition is given for what scale of development counts as "tsunami"-level.
  - The source page pairs the term with self-reported prevention and threat-pipeline statistics (see companion claim-evidence) that are unaudited; the concept's persuasive force partly rests on those unverified numbers.
