summary: 'ReFi Barcelona''s proposed extension of Gitcoin-style quadratic funding (QF), adding a regenerative "Impact Factor" multiplier to the standard QF formula: `Project Allocation = (√Σ contributions²) × (Impact Factor)`. The Impact Factor is stated to combine verified ecological outcomes, social inclusion metrics, governance participation, and knowledge commons contributions — intended to reward measured regenerative effectiveness alongside community preference signals (the raw contribution count QF already captures). Presented as one of four financial-flow-mechanism innovations in the article, alongside "Bioregional Flow Pools" (funding organized into watershed-mirroring Source → Capacity → Project → Yield pools with a cycle-completion loop back to Source) as a structurally related mechanism for the same funding architecture.'
audience: ReFi BCN core team designing funding-round mechanics; allied ReFi/QF practitioners (e.g. Gitcoin-adjacent communities) evaluating impact-weighted variants of quadratic funding.
known_tensions:
  - How the "Impact Factor" is actually computed/verified is not specified in the source — "verified ecological outcomes" and "social inclusion metrics" are named as inputs but no measurement methodology, weighting, or verification process is given.
  - Standard quadratic funding is documented elsewhere (Gitcoin's own published experience) to be vulnerable to collusion/Sybil manipulation of the contribution term; the source does not address whether or how ImpactQF inherits or mitigates this.
  - Multiplying by an unverified Impact Factor risks compounding rather than correcting popularity bias if the factor itself is gameable or poorly calibrated — the source frames this as balancing preference and effectiveness but does not demonstrate the balance holds.
  - No evidence in this source that ImpactQF has been run in a live funding round (contrast with the separate "Catalonia Regenerative Funding Pilot" case study in the same article, which lists "Impact Quadratic Funding" as covering 40% of pilot allocation — see the companion claim-evidence candidate from this ingest for that figure's evidentiary status).
