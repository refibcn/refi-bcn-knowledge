summary: 'A Catalan cooperative-housing model in which a cooperative owns a building collectively and members hold an indefinite right to use their apartment (paying a monthly fee) but cannot sell or profit from it, keeping housing de-commodified. The source describes an ecosystem around this model: Sostre Cívic (founded 2004) is a networked cooperative that develops such projects across Catalonia and onboards resident-members; La Dinamo is a foundation that buys buildings to transfer to housing co-ops, providing technical/financial support; Cal Cases (est. 2007, Bages county) is named as Catalonia''s first co-housing cooperative, converting a farmhouse to communal living. See the companion resource entry for La Borda, the source''s flagship example.'
audience: housing-cooperative organizers and ReFi/commons practitioners comparing de-commodified housing models
known_tensions:
  - collective, non-speculative ownership (no resale/profit for members) vs. the model's continued dependence on city-provided land and government loans/subsidies to be financially viable at scale
