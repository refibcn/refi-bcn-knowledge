Barcelona worker cooperative (8 socias) in social theatre and theatre training, founded 2010 when the pre-existing limited company converted to a cooperativa de treball and folded its teaching team in as worker-members. Since 2015-16 runs the officially accredited Cicle Formatiu de Grau Superior en Tècniques d'Actuació Teatral. Practices Theatre of the Oppressed ("Teatre de les Oprimides"), putting audiences at the centre as active participants; embeds cooperative values transversally in its training programme, emphasising horizontal company organisation over the model of individual professional success.
