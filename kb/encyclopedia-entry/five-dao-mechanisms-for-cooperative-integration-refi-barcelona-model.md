summary: 'ReFi Barcelona''s proposed model for layering DAO mechanisms onto a cooperative structure, built on four hypotheses (transparency builds trust, tokenized incentives widen participation, global connectivity enables bioregional collaboration, technical innovation unlocks regenerative funding) and implemented through five mechanisms: (1) transparent decision-making — a two-layer flow from a public Discourse forum to Snapshot voting to an on-chain Aragon DAO record, then ratified through formal cooperative governance; (2) token-based incentives — a multi-token system (non-transferable Contribution Tokens, Regenerative Action Tokens, a Community Currency, and tokenized Governance Rights) on a layer-2 Ethereum network, explicitly kept subordinate to membership-based economic rights; (3) global connectivity — links to ReFi Mediterranean, Gitcoin, and ReFi DAO balanced against territorial rootedness and decision sovereignty; (4) impact verification and tracking — MRV protocols, a RetrustDAO participatory-verification framework, and bioregional indicators/impact certificates; (5) programmable resource allocation — conviction voting, quadratic funding, regenerative bonding curves, and impact-linked loans, kept under democratic oversight and compliant with cooperative financial regulation.'
audience: ReFi Barcelona members and other bioregional/cooperative nodes evaluating whether and how to adopt DAO tooling without displacing cooperative governance
