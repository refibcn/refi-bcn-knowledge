short_description: A quadratic-funding variant that blends AI-assessed impact scoring with community/donor support in setting the matching split, rather than matching on donor count/amount alone.
source_traditions:
  - Regen Coordination global grants program (GG23, 2025) — first implementation per this source.
toolkit_usage: 'Cited by ReFi BCN (§2.3–2.4) as precedent for its own GG24 proposal: Regen Coordination''s GG23 round distributed $96,000 in matching funds using ImpactQF across 50 projects with 579 unique donors; ReFi Mediterranean''s GG23 localized round applied the model with a 50% AI-impact-score / 50%-community-support matching split across $10,000 and 12 bioregional projects.'
adjacent_meanings:
  - Plain quadratic funding (no AI-impact layer) — the baseline mechanism this variant modifies; see the 'Quadratic Funding' encyclopedia-entry.
important_distinctions:
  - Distinguishes matching-by-donor-count (classic QF) from matching split partly by an AI-generated impact score — a governance choice about who/what sets allocation weight, not just a technical tweak.
tensions:
  - The source treats the AI impact score as a neutral 50% input without describing its methodology, evaluators, or contestability — an unexamined black box in an otherwise transparency-forward pitch.
risks_of_flattening: Presenting ImpactQF's $10,000/12-projects ReFi Med figure and Regen Coordination's $96,000/50-projects/579-donors figure side by side (as this source does) can read as one validated, scaled model when they are two runs at very different scale and, per this source alone, with no shared evaluation of outcomes — treat as two data points, not one proven track record.
