summary: A cooperative legal entity can serve as a "legal wrapper" for a DAO's on-chain activity — providing legal personhood for contracts and property, limited liability for members, recognized banking/financial interfaces, clear jurisdictional grounding, and a path to regulatory compliance — while the DAO continues to experiment with novel, protocol-level governance. The source article ("Why a Cooperative Structure?") frames cooperatives and Web3/DAO governance as sharing a "democratic DNA" (distributed decision-making, member/token-based participation, cooperation-among-cooperatives ≈ protocol interoperability) that makes the wrapper fit more naturally than a conventional corporate form would.
