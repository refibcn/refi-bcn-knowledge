page_type: concept
summary: 'A public-goods funding mechanism in which the matching amount a project receives grows with the square of the sum of the square roots of individual contributions — so many small donations attract disproportionately more matching than one large donation. Framed by this source as a democratizing alternative to top-down grant committees: broad-based community support (not donation size) drives fund allocation, every step of the process is publicly auditable, and local contributions can be multiplied several-fold by matching pools (the source cites a €10,000-local → €30,000-total illustrative ratio).'
audience: local-node-organizers, funders considering participatory rounds
known_tensions:
  - Pitched here as more democratic and transparent than traditional grants, but the source is itself a fundraising pitch — no independent evaluation of QF's failure modes (collusion/Sybil attacks, capture by well-organized donor blocs) is presented.
  - Framed as compatible with cooperative one-person-one-vote principles, but QF weights by number of unique donors, not strictly by people — a donor can be a wallet, complicating the 1p1v analogy.

Source (§2.1–2.2, Catalan: "Finançament Quadràtic") explains the mechanism via the wtfisqf.com primer and contrasts it with traditional grants in a table: top-down selection vs. community decision, fixed evaluation committee vs. open participation, limited vs. total transparency, single vs. multiple funding sources, annual cycles vs. flexibility. This entry generalizes the mechanism definition beyond ReFi BCN's specific GG24 pitch; the pitch itself is captured separately as a resource.
