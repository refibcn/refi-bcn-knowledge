summary: '"Ramaderia extensiva" (extensive livestock farming) names low-intensity grazing systems that work with, rather than intensify against, natural ecosystems — as distinct from confined/industrial livestock production. The source page frames it as a rural-development and ecological- management model at once: it retains population in mountain areas ("population retention"), creates viable rural livelihoods, maintains landscapes (grazing as wildfire-prevention and biodiversity-support tool), and is the subject of a dedicated Catalan regional policy instrument ("Pla Estratègic per la Ramaderia Extensiva a Catalunya"). The Escola de Pastors i Pastores de Catalunya treats training new shepherds ("pastors i pastores") as the delivery mechanism for sustaining the model, embedding it in a broader vision of "pagesia" (traditional Catalan agriculture as a way of life connected to land) and "gestió integral de muntanyes" (integral mountain management — holistic multi-use ecosystem management).'
