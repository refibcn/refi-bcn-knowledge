summary: 'ReFi Barcelona''s four-pillar strategic model, framed as a deliberate progression rather than parallel workstreams: (1) Educació, Recerca i Informació — "from non-knowledge to understanding" — workshops, open repositories of case studies/ecosystem maps, and local toolkits adapting global ReFi/Web3 practice to the ESS (economia social i solidària) context; (2) Comunitat i Xarxa — "from understanding to collaboration" — regular meetups/retreats, mentorship/exchange programs with cooperatives, and sessions with international ReFi experts; (3) Recursos i Capacitat Financera — "from collaboration to tangible impact" — accompanying local projects into regenerative funding (ReFi ecosystems, bioregional finance/ BioFi, Web3 platforms) and advising on sustainable organizational/ financial models, including fundraising counsel (Web3 grants, social currencies, DAOs) and guidance on distributed governance mechanisms; (4) Governança i Innovació — "from consolidation to systemic transformation" — democratic, transparent, distributed governance integrating cooperative and regenerative principles, explicitly marked as a future pillar "to be implemented later, as the ecosystem strengthens." Closes with overarching activities (connecting global ReFi networks to local ESS initiatives; capacity-building for Web3/regenerative-finance adoption; documentation and knowledge-sharing; funding-access events) and a stated purpose: strengthening local ecosocial-transition initiatives'' financial resilience, sustainability, visibility, and impact by integrating global BioFi/ReFi learning into the Barcelona/Catalunya socioeconomic context.'
