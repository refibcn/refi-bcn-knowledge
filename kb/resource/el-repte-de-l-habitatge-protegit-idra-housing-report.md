IDRA research report "El repte de l'habitatge protegit" (The Challenge of Protected Housing) analyzing the social impacts of Barcelona's 30% protected-housing requirement for large rehabilitations and new construction, proposing improvements to implementation without abandoning the 30% measure. No direct link given in the source; the "outcome" (proposals for improving implementation) is IDRA's own self-described framing, not independently verified — treat as the org's stated research output, not a verified policy result.
