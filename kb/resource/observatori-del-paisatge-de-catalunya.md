Catalonia-wide official consortium (Consorcio del Observatorio del Paisaje) for landscape research, planning and protection, based in Olot (Girona, Garrotxa comarca). Institutional/official status (relationship_to_refi_bcn: adjacent per ontology-v1 frontmatter) — not a ReFi-native actor but a reference institution ReFi BCN's bioregional-mapping work could cite or interface with (landscape catalogs, quality objectives, territorial planning tools). Core outputs: Landscape Catalogs (per-territory inventories + quality objectives), a landscape-unit search engine, and active landscape charters/plans across 11 named Catalan territories (Alt Empordà, Alt Penedès, Berguedà, Conca de Barberà, Garraf, Lluçanès, Priorat, Vall de Camprodon, Cerdanya, Cervera, Granollers). Contact details (address/phone/email) are the org's own public institutional contact info, carried from source frontmatter, not treated as a high-risk exact-location trigger (a public consortium's office address, not land tenure or a sensitive site).
