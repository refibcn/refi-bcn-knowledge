Barcelona City Council's Citizen Climate Assembly (ACC), a sortition-selected 100-resident body (16-75 y/o) run June 2022 - January 2023 under the 2020 Climate Emergency Declaration. Produced 36 draft proposals across three axes (Energy, Mobility, Consumption & Waste); 34 passed the >=51% agreement / >=50% turnout threshold. Governed by an Oversight Commission (civil servants, all 7 council party groups, FAVB, youth council, XES, Greenpeace, XR, Ecologistes en Acció, climate-justice NGOs, 5 elected assembly members) that per the source was slated to audit implementation twice a year through 2025. Proposals cover concrete municipal-governance recommendations (energy-tariff design, rent-cap-by-energy-rating, LEZ replacement scheme) — flagged high_risk as governance/policy recommendations even though the underlying report is already public via the Ajuntament. Full 34-proposal list and voting thresholds are in the source; not re-enumerated here to keep this object small — see the source file / linked PDF for the complete catalogue by axis (E1-E10, M1-M11, C1-C13). high_risk trigger: governance/policy recommendations (municipal-level).
