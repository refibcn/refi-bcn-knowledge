Music-teacher worker cooperative (EMP SCCL, carrer de Sant Pere més Alt 6, 08003 Barcelona; 3 worker-members, 2 employees), founded 1985-86 when the music school born in 1975-76 as "Escola de Música de l'Orfeó Català" became independent from the Orfeó and converted to a cooperative. Contact: info@escolademusicadelpalau.com, 932 682 510. Practices cooperative learning among students: since 1991 runs an annual collective-creation process across all language groups, producing a student-written/illustrated cantata. In 2018, in partnership with the Jane Goodall Institute's "Mobilitza't per la selva" environmental- education campaign, dedicated the cantata ("El viatge de la Jane") to mobile-phone reuse/recycling awareness to protect African ecosystems.
