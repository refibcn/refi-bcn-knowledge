Non-profit foundation providing patient, non-speculative financing to federated worker cooperatives and social-economy entities in Catalonia. Main supporter: Federació de Cooperatives de Treball de Catalunya (Federation of Worker Cooperatives of Catalonia); Financial Orientation program executed by Seira Impuls Cooperatiu. Programs named in the source: Seira Inversió Social (patient/sustainable financing), Més Espais (financing for social facilities), Financial Orientation (advisory), Formació (training), Financoop. Mission per source (Catalan): "Les finances al servei de les persones i les cooperatives."
