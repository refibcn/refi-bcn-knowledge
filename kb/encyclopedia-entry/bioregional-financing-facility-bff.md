summary: 'A BioFi-framework institution that supports a bioregion''s transition to an economy less dependent on financial capital overall (and on capital imported from outside the region), aligning financial flows with real flows of value. Works at multiple nested levels — technical assistance to regenerative organizations, market development, pooled funding for common assets, capital-raising, derisking, and integrated MRV strategies. The guide recommends four core templates that can build on one another in succession: (1) a Bioregional Trust — a catalytic grant fund raising philanthropic/public grants and donations, allocating to Bioregional Hubs, Organizing Teams, and priority projects; (2) a Bioregional Venture Studio — a non-profit/PBC/co-op/steward-owned entity/DAO incubating cohorts of synergistic early-stage regenerative organizations to drive systems change; (3) an Investment Company — a PBC/co-op/steward-owned entity/DAO developing a portfolio of Systemic Investment Funds and Bioregional Regeneration Bonds using an integrated-capital approach; (4) a Bioregional Bank — provides low-interest loans, microloans, lines of credit, and retail banking, funded by guarantees/deposits from bioregional citizens, and can issue a complementary or nature-based currency. A BFF is one of three synergistic "legs of a stool" alongside the Bioregional Organizing Team and the Bioregional Hub.'
audience: Bioregional organizers and practitioners scoping or designing a financing facility for a bioregion (e.g. as a reference for ReFi BCN's own Catalunya/Mediterranean bioregional work).
