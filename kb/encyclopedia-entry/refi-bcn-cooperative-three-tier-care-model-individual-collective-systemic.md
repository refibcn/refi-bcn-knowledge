Individual care — regular 1:1 check-ins on wellbeing/needs, flexible schedules (in-person/remote), support for training and professional development, public recognition of contributions. Collective care — monthly assemblies with space to share and listen, quarterly retreats for connection and strategic planning, celebration of milestones, conflict-resolution protocols. Systemic care — progressive distribution of responsibility and decision power, creation of diverse roles enabling different modes of participation, development of shared/rotating leadership, integration of restorative-justice principles into governance.'
