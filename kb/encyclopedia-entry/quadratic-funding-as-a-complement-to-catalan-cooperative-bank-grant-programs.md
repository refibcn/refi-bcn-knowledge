summary: 'A framing, drafted by ReFi BCN for its GG24 (Gitcoin Grants Round 24) outreach, arguing that quadratic funding (QF) complements — rather than replaces — the grant/social-investment programs already run by Catalan cooperative banks (e.g. Coop57, Fiare Banca Etica). The pitch: QF extends "one member, one vote" cooperative democratic principles to funding allocation, adds public transparency and automated impact reporting, and lets a bank''s contribution be multiplied by international matching pools (framed as "€10,000 could become €30,000"). Proposes a low-risk pilot (small amounts, local evaluation criteria, ReFi BCN handling the technical layer) building on a track record claimed from a prior round ("GG23").'
audience: Cooperative bank program officers and social-investment committees in Catalonia evaluating whether to co-fund or match a Gitcoin-style quadratic-funding round.
known_tensions:
  - Presents QF's "community-driven, fully transparent" framing against traditional grant-making's "top-down, limited transparency" framing as a clean binary (see the source's comparison table); cooperative banks may see their existing participatory mechanisms as already democratic.
  - The multiplier-effect pitch (external matching pools) depends on continued availability of Gitcoin-ecosystem matching funds, which is outside any Catalan bank's control.
