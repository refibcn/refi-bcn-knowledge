summary: 'A framework for how a local bioregional node (e.g. ReFi Barcelona) engages with successively wider systems without losing bioregional autonomy: Local (ReFi Barcelona) participates in Regional (ReFi Mediterranean), which connects to Global (ReFi DAO); the local node also connects directly to Thematic (e.g. RegenNetwork) and Sectoral (e.g. cooperative networks) scales, while the regional layer interfaces with Institutional bodies (e.g. UNEP Mediterranean) and the global layer with Global Systems (e.g. UNFCCC). The central hypothesis: regenerative efforts achieve greater systemic impact when local initiatives connect through networks that mirror larger ecological patterns, rather than acting in isolation or centralizing into a single hierarchy. Four collaboration patterns instantiate the framework at the Mediterranean-regional scale: knowledge commoning, resource pooling, market network development, and governance collaboration.'
