Broad-based citizens' platform (since 2011) campaigning for public, commons-based water management in Catalonia, uniting neighborhood groups, ecologists, and social movements toward remunicipalization of urban water systems. Source cites Terrassa (215,000 residents) reclaiming its water service in 2018 after 75 years of private control, and a resulting co-governance "Terrassa Water Observatory"; also describes unresolved pressure on Barcelona's private concession (Agbar). Kept faithful to the source's framing of this as historical/ongoing citizen advocacy, not restated as a settled outcome for Barcelona. Not flagged high_risk: this is a reporting summary of public, well-documented municipal-water advocacy history, not a funding/legal/governance recommendation being made by this ingest.
