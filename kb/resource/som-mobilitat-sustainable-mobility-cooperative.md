Catalan non-profit consumer cooperative (founded circa 2016) offering electric car-sharing to members, aiming to accelerate a shared-fleet transition to sustainable mobility. Source notes it often collaborates with city governments and housing cooperatives to set up electric-car hubs, linking mobility to community energy (charging with green electricity) and the wider social economy.
