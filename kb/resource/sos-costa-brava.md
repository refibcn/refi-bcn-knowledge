Non-profit federation of 25+ Catalan civic/environmental entities (founded August 2018) defending the Costa Brava (Girona coastline, 22 municipalities) against urban speculation and major infrastructure projects, through legal action ("Defensa jurídica"), public campaigns ("Denúncia pública"), and social mobilization ("Mobilització social"). HQ: Carrer del Progrés 15, 17200 Palafrugell, Girona — a public promotional/contact address, low sensitivity. Flagship campaign "Construir és Destruir"; also runs an electoral-accountability campaign, a "Conservatori del Litoral" coastal- protection program, environmental-education programs (incl. a "Premi SOS Costa Brava Educació" prize), and site-specific "Zones amenaçades" (threatened-zones) monitoring. Partners named on the source page include IAEDEN-Salvem l'Empordà (partner federation member — held out, no independent profile in this ingest).
