summary: A quadratic-funding evaluation methodology that blends traditional community-support metrics (donor counts, contribution patterns) with AI-generated impact scores, splitting matching-fund allocation between the two (e.g. 50/50 in the ReFi Mediterranean GG23 pilot). The Regen Coordination Global GG23 round scaled the approach using GPT-4o and Claude 3.7 to generate structured evaluation reports, and paired it with the Common Impact Data Standard (CIDS) via Karma GAP so the resulting impact data is machine-readable at scale. Framed by the source document as a template other localized/bioregional rounds (including a proposed GG24 Catalunya round) can adapt.
