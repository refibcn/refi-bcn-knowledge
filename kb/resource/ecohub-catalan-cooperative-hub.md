Catalan cooperative hub ("pol cooperatiu") of 100+ professionals organized into nine member entities, working with citizens, organizations, businesses, and public administrations to accelerate "just and democratic ecological transitions" ("Accelerant transicions"). Uses Doughnut Economics (Kate Raworth) as its guiding theoretical framework. Ten strategic/thematic lines cited in source: futures, democracy, transitions, innovation, research, justice, resilience, training, culture, planning; four service methodologies: accompaniment, facilitation, knowledge transfer, resource provision. Headquartered at BLOC4BCN cooperative space, Carrer de la Constitució 19, Sants-Montjuïc, 08014 Barcelona (public office address published on the org's own materials, not a residence). Contact: info@ecohub.cat.
