Formative project (managed by Associació rurbans, ~15 years old, est. 2008-2009) training new shepherds and promoting extensive livestock farming ("ramaderia extensiva") as a rural-development model in the Catalan Pyrenees. Admin offices at Crta. C13 km 131, 25594 Rialp (Lleida); training center in Poble d'Enviny, municipality of Sort (Pallars Sobirà). Contact: info@rurbans.org, 973 620 977. Ontology-v1 frontmatter: type educational, status active, focus_areas [shepherding, extensive-livestock, rural-development, pastoral, pyrenees], network_affiliation [Associació rurbans], relationship_to_refi_bcn: adjacent, last_verified 2026-05-06. Runs training (basic + advanced), administrative/policy consultation, farm -succession accompaniment ("traspassos"), an agricultural test space ("Espai Test Agrari"), a job/opportunity board ("Borsa d'oportunitats"), and contributes to the "Pla Estratègic per la Ramaderia Extensiva a Catalunya" (regional strategic plan). high_risk: the school's stated method explicitly includes documenting and transmitting "traditional pastoral knowledge" and "elder knowledge" from experienced shepherds/farmers (TEK-adjacent) — see accompanying public-use-boundary.
