important_distinctions:

  - Phase 1 entities (Trust, Venture Studio) are grant/incubation-facing; Phase 2 entities (Investment Company, Bank) are capital-market-facing — collapsing the two phases into one launch is a common misreading the source's phasing is meant to prevent
