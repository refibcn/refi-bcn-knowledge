summary: A change-methodology framework, developed by Bill Sharpe and colleagues, for thinking about the present ("Horizon 1," the existing system), emerging alternatives ("Horizon 2," transition patterns), and a desired future ("Horizon 3," renewed patterns of life) as three overlapping perspectives held at once rather than a single linear roadmap. Surfaced here via Gaia Education's "The Patterning of Hope" program (with Bill Sharpe and Yasu Mali), which uses it as a "perspective of hope" tool for groups navigating systemic transformation, and which the source page links explicitly to bioregional-organizing-team activation work.
