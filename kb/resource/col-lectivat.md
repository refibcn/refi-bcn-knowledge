Col·lectivaT SCCL (constituted 2018), Barcelona (Call). Non-profit worker cooperative combining cultural-translation services (Catalan↔Turkish, plus Kurdish/Spanish/English/Arabic/French) with language technology (computational linguistics, speech recognition/transcription, machine translation) aimed at minoritized languages, and social research/consulting in Turkey/Kurdistan and Spain/Catalonia. Explicitly frames its work as "procomú" (commons) — building and maintaining shared knowledge-worker resources, open data repositories, and free/open-source tools — and champions "sobirania tecnològica" (technological sovereignty): independence from proprietary software and monopolistic knowledge channels. Cooperative is composed largely of migrated workers (predominantly Turkish-origin), positioned by the source as a pioneering case in Catalonia for diverse/ intercultural solidarity economy alongside DiomCoop, Alencop, CoopHalal, and MigrESS. Relevant to ReFi BCN's own bridging of cooperative and digital-commons framing (open tooling, data sovereignty) even though the org itself is not (yet) a known ReFi BCN relationship.
