Spain's first renewable-energy cooperative, founded 2010 at the University of Girona; sells green electricity to members and invests in local renewable projects. Source frames it as a demonstration of "energy as a commons" through cooperative ownership and democratic governance; spread from Girona to Barcelona and beyond. Source (a 2024/2025-vintage secondary report proposing "new entries" for a Knowledge Commons spreadsheet) gives no URL or member-count figure as of this ingest; link_status: unresolved pending an operator lookup rather than an invented URL.
